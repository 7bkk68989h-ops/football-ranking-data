# Football Ranking – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 4 November 2026*

Football Ranking itself does not collect, store or share any personal data. On iPhone and iPad, the free version shows ads from Google AdMob, described below.

- **No account.** You do not need to sign up or log in.
- **No analytics.** The app contains no analytics tools, and does not use your location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
- **Ads (iPhone and iPad).** The free version shows a banner from Google AdMob. The app asks whether you allow tracking (Apple's App Tracking Transparency): if you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads that fit you better; if you don't, you get non-personalised ads, and the app works the same. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your IP address (used for approximate location), device identifiers, and how you interact with the ad, as described in [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app first asks for your consent with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy Choices. A one-off in-app purchase, "Remove Ads", removes the ads on all your devices; if you bought the app before it became free, you never see ads. There are no ads on Apple TV.
- **Purchases.** Purchases are handled by Apple. The app only learns whether "Remove Ads" was bought and when this Apple Account first got the app; it receives no payment details.
- **Settings stay on your device.** Your chosen language and other preferences are saved only on your device.
- **Data downloads.** When it opens, the app downloads a public data file (club trophies) from GitHub. The request contains no information about you. As with any website, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address in its server logs; see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Ratings.** The app may ask you to rate it using Apple's standard rating prompt. That is handled by Apple, and the app does not receive your answer.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages but is not directed at children.
