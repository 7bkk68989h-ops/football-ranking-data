# Football Ranking – Support

Football Ranking ranks football clubs by the trophies they have won, in every country, every continent and the world.

## Contact

Questions, suggestions, or found a title or result that looks wrong? Email **[apps.iloveideas@gmail.com](mailto:apps.iloveideas@gmail.com)**. Please include the club, the competition and the season, so it can be checked and fixed in the next data update.

## Frequently asked questions

**Where does the data come from?**
From [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org) (CC0) and [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org) (CC BY-SA 4.0), with manual corrections. It is refreshed regularly and the app downloads the latest version when it opens, without an app update.

**How are the rankings calculated?**
Each ranking has a "How the ranking works" page in the app, listing the competitions it counts and the points given to each one.

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes. It uses the last data it downloaded, or the data included with the app.

Football Ranking is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any club, league, federation or confederation.
